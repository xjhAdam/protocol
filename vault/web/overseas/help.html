How to Use the Vault Feature

Step 1: Set a Vault Password

When entering the vault for the first time, you will be prompted to set a fingerprint password, gesture password, or text password. This password is used to protect your hidden content.

Tip: Choose a secure but memorable password to prevent others from accessing your private data.

Step 2: Grant Required Permissions

To ensure the vault works properly, please grant required permissions such as file access, photo access, and storage permissions.

Step 3: Add Content to Hide

Inside the vault, you can choose content to hide, including photos, videos, audio, documents, and APK files. Once added, these files will be hidden from the system and only visible inside the vault.

Step 4: Securely Manage Your Private Files

After adding files, all hidden content is securely protected. Only users who enter the correct password can access and manage these files.

You can change your password or restore hidden files at any time in settings.
